👋 About me
I'm Majid (MJ) Allahverdi, a Senior Full-Stack Developer in Toronto with 14+ years of designing and shipping production systems across healthcare, e-commerce, and SaaS.
My home turf is .NET and Azure with Clean Architecture, DDD, and CQRS, paired with Next.js/React on the web and Flutter on mobile. I build systems that stay maintainable under real load, real compliance requirements, and real budgets.
🏥 Lead engineer at Beeplife — a PHIPA/PIPEDA-compliant, FHIR-aware healthcare platform on .NET 10, Next.js, Flutter and Azure. Owned the full stack end-to-end; currently driving SOC 2 readiness.
🤖 Applied AI — AI-assisted clinical data processing, voice AI (ElevenLabs), OpenAI and Anthropic APIs, plus tooling like `llm-token-formats-benchmark` and `n8n-nodes-json-sanitizer`
📦 Open source — `UniversalNotifier.NET`, `Block.IO.Core`
☁️ Azure cost optimization — right-sizing, tagging policies and cost allocation that gave a startup real cloud-spend visibility
💬 Open to senior and lead roles, remote or hybrid, contract or permanent → majidalahverdi@gmail.com
